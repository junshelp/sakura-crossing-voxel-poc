## Summary

Replaced the Voxel tracer boxes with deterministic sparse Voxel Assets compiled
through the W002 validator/compiler and Three.js conversion boundary. The slice

## Acceptance Evidence

- Assets cover crossing equipment/barriers/lights, train car shell, shop, both
  house variants, vending machine, relay box, utility pole, fence, cherry tree,
  parked bicycle, marker, and restrained petal treatment.
- Asset construction is deterministic, canonical-grid aligned, bounded, and
  validated at creation. Details overwrite shell cells through a coordinate map,
  so every output has unique occupancy and stable coordinate ordering.
- All occupied voxel surfaces reach Three.js through `compileVoxelAsset` and
  `surfaceGroupToBufferGeometry`. No occupied cell constructs a runtime Mesh,
  Object3D, material, or texture.
- Repeated cherry trees share compiled geometry identities; train cars share the
  train-car compilation. Every chunk has computed bounds and frustum culling.
- Opaque, transparent, and emissive chunks use bounded shared material paths.
  All three sign planes share exactly one nearest-filtered DataTexture atlas and
  one atlas material. Petals use one entity-owned Points object.
- Crossing barrier pivots and warning-light nodes contain visible compiled mesh
  descendants. Directional bounds tests prove the east arm occupies negative
  local x and the west arm positive local x from their pivots. Exactly two named
  train cars and child pivots are retained under the train root; their rigid
  roots remain seated on the sphere.
- `data-voxel-entity-count`, `data-voxel-chunk-mesh-count`,
  `data-voxel-unique-material-count`, `data-voxel-atlas-texture-count`, and
  `data-renderer-draw-calls` are exposed for browser inspection. The e2e smoke
  observed Baseline draw calls of 58 and fewer Voxel draw calls in the same
  session while preserving player and Continuous Infrastructure state.

## Assumptions or Deviations

- The implementation is in `src/renderers/voxel/index.ts`; the legacy-compatible
  `src/adapters.ts` module only re-exports the public adapter boundary.
- The compact atlas is a deterministic generated DataTexture rather than a
  source image. It is intentionally small and shared by all sign planes.
- Warning lenses are represented by emissive compiled cells under stable named
  warning nodes; W006 owns their state changes and train/barrier simulation.
- Draw-call reduction is preliminary W005 evidence only; W007 owns warm-up,
  percentile sampling, thresholds, and final conclusions. Human Visual
  Acceptance remains pending.
