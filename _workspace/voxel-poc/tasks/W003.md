## Required Behavior

- Define one `PLANET_RADIUS` of 160 m and derive `PLANET_CIRCUMFERENCE` as
  `2 * PI * radius` rather than duplicating approximately 1,005 m.
- Author flat coordinates with +x east along the railway, +y up, and +z north.
  Map flat x to longitude and flat z to latitude so flat origin is the top of the
  sphere, the x tangent is east, and the z tangent is north.
- Provide pure functions for wrapped x, surface basis, surface position including
  local height, flat point projection, and rigid-object seating. Keep all planet
  math in `src/world`.
- The local east/up/north basis must be right-handed and orthonormal.
- Flat origin at height zero must map to world origin with the planet center at
  y = -160. Points on the equatorial railway must remain one radius from center.
- Provide a static BufferGeometry projection function that preserves non-position
  attributes, recomputes normals/bounds, and documents that callers must segment
  long geometry before projection. Do not silently subdivide W002 voxel buffers.
- Rigid seating must preserve a supplied local rotation and scale while aligning
  the object's local axes to the surface basis.
- Expand the Shared Scene Specification into the fixed Miniature Crossing Slice.
  Include world settings, populated flat bounds, deterministic seed, benchmark
  camera markers with positions and look-at targets, stable semantic entities,
  optional colliders, interactions, palette roles, animation bindings, and visual
  archetype/variant data sufficient for later adapters.
- Represent the complete approved inventory semantically even if W003 renders
  only tracers: one crossing, one two-car train entity or one train consist with
  explicit car count, one shop, two distinct houses, one vending machine, one
  relay box, utility poles and wires, fencing, four to six cherry trees, restrained
  petals, and one parked bicycle or vehicle. Keep all populated positions inside
  the 60 x 60 m flat bounds.
- The vending machine and relay box own the approved interaction definitions in
  the Shared Scene Specification. The crossing and train own animation bindings.
  Renderer adapters must not define colliders, interactions, or simulation state.
- Build shared Continuous Infrastructure outside both adapters: visible planet,
  curved road/footway tracer, and a visibly closed equatorial railway loop with
  rails. Segment projected strips/curves densely enough to follow the sphere.
- Continuous Infrastructure must expose a disposal boundary and stable object
  names for inspection.
- Both placeholder adapters must still receive the same immutable scene object;
  switching modes preserves that object and the shared infrastructure.
- Position the camera from a benchmark marker so the browser tracer visibly shows
  a curved horizon, road, local rails, and content markers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Unit tests verify origin mapping, planet center/radius, circumference wrapping,
  equatorial closure, representative latitude mapping, and local-height offset.
- Basis vectors have unit length, pairwise near-zero dot products, and
  `east x up = north` within tolerance at origin and representative coordinates.
- Rigid seating tests verify position, local rotation composition, scale, and
  preserved child pivot relationships.
- Static geometry projection preserves a custom attribute, outputs finite values,
  and computes bounding box/sphere.
- Contract tests reject duplicate entity ids, out-of-bounds populated entities,
  invalid collider dimensions, invalid interaction ownership, and missing camera
  markers through a public validator or assertion function.
- Tests prove both adapters receive the same Shared Scene Specification and do not
  own colliders or interactions.
- Continuous Infrastructure tests or stable inspection hooks prove one shared
  planet, road/footway set, and closed two-rail loop exist outside adapter roots.
- Existing W001/W002 tests remain green.
- Browser smoke test still switches modes and observes stable shared
  infrastructure before and after the switch.
